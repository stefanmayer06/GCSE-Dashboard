# Age, children and education statement

## Product position

GCSE Study Desk is intended for learners aged **13 and over**, primarily teenagers preparing for GCSE study. It is not designed or marketed for children under 13. It is a general consumer education app, not a school-controlled student-information system.

The app uses email/password accounts, stores linked learning progress, and offers optional AI tutor and English feedback. Learners are told not to enter names, contact details, school details or sensitive information into free-text responses. AI output may be inaccurate and is not an official grade.

GCSE Study Desk is independent and is not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by AQA.
