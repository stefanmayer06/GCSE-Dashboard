# Store screenshot plan

Capture the production build on physical or store-approved simulated devices. Use a fictional confirmed demo account and synthetic answers only.

## Visual sequence

1. **Home:** overall status across all three study areas with a clear next step. Caption: `Know what to revise next.`
2. **Foundation desk:** Trail Indigo study area with readiness, week plan and mastery trail. Caption: `Foundation has its own desk.`
3. **Higher desk:** Pine study area with Higher papers and grade 4–9 context. Caption: `Higher stays separate.`
4. **English desk:** Ember study area with source library and writing tasks. Caption: `Read, write and improve.`
5. **Learn:** recognisable topic list for one course. Caption: `Course notes, kept focused.`
6. **Practice setup:** session choices and paper ticket detail. Caption: `Practise the format you need.`
7. **Question:** an original, readable question with navigation and progress. Caption: `Draft answers stay on your device.`
8. **Results:** useful review and next step, with no claim of an official grade. Caption: `Turn each attempt into a next step.`
9. **Tutor:** a short guided exchange containing no personal information. Caption: `Ask for the method, not just the answer.`
10. **Dark mode:** one desk in its night mood with subject hues brightened. Caption: `Alive after 11pm revision — just gentler.`

## Capture matrix

- Apple: supply current required iPhone and, because `supportsTablet` is true, iPad screenshot sets shown in App Store Connect. Capture native layouts rather than scaling phone images.
- Google: provide at least the required phone set and add tablet sets if distributing to tablets. Include the 1024 x 500 feature graphic separately.
- Capture light and dark examples, but keep each sequence visually coherent.
- Keep status bars tidy, text legible and tap targets unobscured. Do not use device frames that imply unsupported hardware.
- Do not show email addresses, tokens, school names, copyrighted exam-paper scans, provider logos without permission, or “official AQA” language.
- Verify exact pixel/count rules in each console at upload time; console requirements can change independently of this repository.
