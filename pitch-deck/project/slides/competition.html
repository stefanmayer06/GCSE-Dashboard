How learners revise today

Alternative Good at Where it leaves a learner
Revision sites such as Maths Genie, Seneca and Save My Exams Large free libraries of questions, notes and papers Built for breadth across subjects and boards; the learner still decides what to fix next
School platforms such as Sparx Maths Weekly homework set and tracked by teachers Bought by schools and paced to the class, not to one learner’s exam date
Private tutors Personal feedback and accountability Typically £25–£40 an hour, and access varies by region and income
General AI chatbots Instant explanations of any topic Not shaped to AQA papers, marks and timing, and no retry schedule
GCSE Study Desk Exam-date plan, timed practice and marking in the open Every miss is tagged, retried on day 1, 3, 7 and 21, and proven mastered

Sources: Tutorperch, 2026 · Sutton Trust, Private Tutoring 2026
