Make mistakes extinct.

Revisaurus is the proposed name for the product now called GCSE Study Desk: revise plus thesaurus, with a dinosaur's roar. It says what the app is for in its first two syllables and fits the study creatures already in the app. No app, company, business or domain was found using it. The mark is unchanged.

The name

Revise, plus a word everyone can already spell

Revisaurus follows the pattern of the names you like. Duolingo is duo plus lingo and Emofest is emo plus fest: a plain meaning with a playful twist. Here the meaning is revise and the twist is thesaurus, a word students already know how to say and spell, so the name sticks after one hearing.

Revise

The job, in the first two syllables. Nobody has to ask what the app is for.

Thesaurus

The rhythm comes from a word every student knows, so the name is easy to say and spell after hearing it once. A thesaurus is also a book of words, a nod to English.

-saurus

Big, loud and friendly. It joins the study creatures already in the app: Rexam the T. rex, Memmoth the mammoth and Redo the phoenix chick.

Make mistakes extinct

The promise in four words. Every miss comes back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days until it is gone for good.

A twist on a known word

Revisaurus

Duolingo, Kahoot and Emofest all start from familiar words. A twist on a known word is quicker to remember than a made-up one, and still owned by no one.

What that means for the brand

Stay away from the bolt

Save My Exams' logo is a lightning bolt and Seneca's hero uses ⚡. The volt colour already carries the energy, so the mark uses the quadrant disc instead.

Sell the loop, not the bank

Free papers and questions are everywhere (Maths Genie, Corbettmaths, PMT, Bitesize). The only story here that competitors can't copy by adding content is the mistake-to-mastery loop, and the closing disc shows it.

Be the game, not the library

Seneca, Save My Exams and Medly all open on white with flat blue or black, and none of them leads with a character. Their gamification is a feature, not the brand. Revisaurus makes it the brand, with Pip, the creatures and the night stage.

What makes a name stick

Round three started from the research on memorable brand names rather than from word lists.

Two or three beats

Names of two or three syllables are remembered better than longer ones, and a strong stress helps (GOO-gle, CO-ca-CO-la). Revisaurus has four, like Duolingo, and gets away with it because its rhythm is borrowed from thesaurus.

Echoes stick

Repeated sounds make a name more noticeable, more memorable and better liked, especially when it is said aloud. Revisaurus repeats its r and s sounds.

Twist a word people know

Kahoot came from "cahoots", and Duolingo from duo and lingo. A twist on a known word is learnt in one hearing. The short made-up names were all taken: Fixfox, Unoops, Tickety, Examigo, Redoku and Gradiator are already registered.

Say what it does

The strongest names in this market hint at the job: Save My Exams, Quizlet, Duolingo. Revisaurus starts with it.
