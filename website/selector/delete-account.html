What deletion does

Deletion is intended to permanently remove your Supabase authentication account, username/user ID mapping, saved Maths and English progress, XP, streaks, completed lessons, responses/results retained by the service, tutor history retained by the service, and active server study/authentication sessions.

This cannot be undoneYou will lose server progress across all subjects. A new account will not recover it.

Encrypted backups and narrowly necessary security or audit records may take time to expire where immediate selective deletion is technically impracticable or retention is legally required. They must not be used to restore your account as active data. Device-local drafts, results, preferences or tutor transcripts are controlled by your device and may remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it; account deletion prevents them from reconnecting to the deleted account.

Delete now

This page sends your email and password only to the same-origin sign-in endpoint. The returned bearer token is held in a JavaScript variable only for the deletion request, then discarded. Nothing is written to cookies, local storage or session storage by this page.

If you cannot authenticate or need a rights request handled another way, use the support route.
