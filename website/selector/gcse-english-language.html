04 / Questions

English revision FAQ

What is in AQA English Language Paper 1?

Paper 1, Explorations in creative reading and writing, lasts 1 hour 45 minutes and uses one unseen fiction source. Four reading questions test retrieval, language, structure and evaluation, followed by a 40-mark creative writing task.

What is in AQA English Language Paper 2?

Paper 2, Writers' viewpoints and perspectives, also lasts 1 hour 45 minutes. It uses two linked non-fiction sources for retrieval, summary, language and comparison questions, followed by a 40-mark viewpoint writing task.

How can I improve English Language analysis?

Start with a precise idea about the text, choose short evidence that genuinely supports it, name a method only when useful, and explain how the wording or structure shapes a reader's understanding. Then compare your paragraph with the rubric.

Is GCSE Study Desk an official AQA website?

No. GCSE Study Desk is an independent revision product and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by AQA. AI-assisted feedback is revision guidance, not an official grade.
