04 / Questions

Foundation revision FAQ

What does AQA GCSE Maths Foundation cover?

The tier covers Number, Algebra, Ratio, proportion and rates of change, Geometry and measures, Probability, and Statistics. Foundation candidates can achieve grades 1 to 5.

Which papers can I practise?

You can practise Paper 1 without a calculator and Papers 2 and 3 with a calculator. Full papers use 80 marks and 90 minutes; shorter sessions use 40 marks and 45 minutes.

Should I revise topics or complete papers?

Use both. Topic practice repairs a known gap efficiently, while timed papers test whether you can choose and apply methods when the topic is not labelled for you.

Is GCSE Study Desk an official AQA website?

No. GCSE Study Desk is an independent revision product and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by AQA.
