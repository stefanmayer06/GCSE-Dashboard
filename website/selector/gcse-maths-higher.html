01 / Approach

Higher practice with clear evidence

Higher Maths questions ask you to connect ideas, select a method independently and communicate a convincing chain of reasoning. MathsMate combines focused drills with original paper-style questions, exact marking and worked solutions so you can see what changed between attempts.

02 / Coverage

Higher Maths topics

Higher practice draws from the shared Maths topics and adds selected Higher algebra, geometry, probability and proof questions. Your Higher progress stays separate from Foundation work.

Number and Algebra

  • Standard form, indices, surds and bounds
  • Quadratic and simultaneous equations
  • Function values and algebraic fractions
  • Straight-line graphs and introductory proof

Ratio and graphs

  • Repeated percentage growth and decay
  • Inverse-square proportion
  • Gradients and quadratic graph reading
  • Scale factors and similar shapes

Geometry and trigonometry

  • Cosine rule and triangle area formula
  • Angle-at-centre theorem and sectors
  • Vector components
  • Right-angle trigonometry and transformations

Probability and Statistics

  • Without-replacement trees and independent events
  • Histograms and cumulative frequency
  • Frequency density and grouped data
  • Interquartile range from box plots

This is a growing practice selection, not a checklist of every specification statement. Check the full AQA 8300 content for your course requirements.

04 / Questions

Higher revision FAQ

What grades does this GCSE Maths Higher course target?

The Higher course is designed for grades 4 to 9. Its questions, recommendations and saved progress stay separate from Foundation tier.

What is different about Higher revision?

Higher revision includes the shared GCSE Maths foundations plus content such as surds, functions, proof, advanced trigonometry, vectors and conditional probability. It also demands more unfamiliar and connected problem solving.

How should I use Higher paper practice?

Complete original timed practice papers to rehearse selection and pacing, then turn every lost mark into a named topic and mistake reason. Repair that method with focused questions before attempting another paper.

Is GCSE Study Desk an official AQA website?

No. GCSE Study Desk is an independent revision product and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by AQA.
