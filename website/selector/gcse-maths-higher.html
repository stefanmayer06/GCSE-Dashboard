02 / Coverage

Higher Maths topics

Higher revision includes every shared GCSE Maths strand and extends them with more abstract algebra, geometry, probability and proof. The practice bank keeps Higher progress separate from Foundation work.

Number and Algebra

  • Surds, indices and recurring decimals
  • Quadratics and simultaneous equations
  • Functions, iteration and algebraic fractions
  • Sequences, inequalities and proof

Ratio and graphs

  • Direct and inverse proportion
  • Velocity-time and rate-of-change graphs
  • Growth, decay and compound measures
  • Gradients and areas under curves

Geometry and trigonometry

  • Sine and cosine rules
  • Circle theorems and constructions
  • Vectors and geometric proof
  • Transformations and similarity

Probability and Statistics

  • Conditional probability and tree diagrams
  • Histograms and cumulative frequency
  • Box plots and data comparison
  • Sampling and statistical reasoning

04 / Questions

Higher revision FAQ

What grades does this GCSE Maths Higher course target?

The Higher course is designed for grades 4 to 9. Its questions, recommendations and saved progress stay separate from Foundation tier.

What is different about Higher revision?

Higher revision includes the shared GCSE Maths foundations plus content such as surds, functions, proof, advanced trigonometry, vectors and conditional probability. It also demands more unfamiliar and connected problem solving.

How should I use Higher past-paper practice?

Complete timed papers to practise selection and pacing, then turn every lost mark into a named topic and mistake reason. Repair that method with focused questions before attempting another paper.

Is GCSE Study Desk an official AQA website?

No. GCSE Study Desk is an independent revision product and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by AQA.
