Cannot sign in

  1. Check that you used the email address attached to your account.
  2. Check for spaces and try the password-reset link from the sign-in screen.
  3. Do not create another account if you need progress from the first one.

Content or progress will not load

  1. Check your internet connection. Starting activities, marking and tutor replies need one.
  2. Reload the page or app, then try again.
  3. Check the selected course. Maths Foundation, Maths Higher and English keep separate progress.
  4. Avoid clearing app data while a draft is unsent.

Saved drafts and missing results

Practice drafts and recent result views may remain on one device until submitted or cleared. A session that has expired on the server may remain visible locally but cannot always be accepted for marks or XP.

Tutor or English feedback

AI-assisted features need a connection and may be unavailable when the provider is down. AI feedback can be wrong: use it as revision guidance, not an official grade or examiner decision.

Send a support request

GCSE Study Desk is operated by Mayer Digital. Use this form for support or privacy requests.

Tell us the course and screen involved, what happened and what you expected. Leave a reply email if you want an answer. A reply email is needed for privacy requests. Do not send passwords, access tokens, full answers or sensitive personal details.

Requests are stored privately for review. Our retention target is 180 days; a monthly maintenance check removes older requests. If you leave an email, we use it only to reply about this request. For account deletion, use the deletion page.
